# Phase 1: Legal Thinking Format

## Goal

Teach the model to generate legal moves inside a forced `<think>...</think>` block without forgetting normal play.

## Format (Forced)

```
Input:  <e2e4>
Output: <think> <c7c5> <g1f3> <d2d4> </think> <e7e5>
```

- `<think>` and `</think>` are injected (not generated by the model)
- Model fills in up to `N` move tokens inside the thinking block
- Model generates exactly 1 move after `</think>` (the played move)
- No `<branch>` token in this phase

## Sampling

- `N` (max thinking tokens) sampled from `randint(2, 8)` per example
- If model emits fewer than `N` tokens, pad/truncate as needed

## Reward (Outcome-based)

Single scalar per generation, GRPO-compatible:

```
R = (legal_thinking_moves / N) + (played_move_legal ? 0.5 : 0.0)
```

- `legal_thinking_moves`: count of consecutive legal moves from the start (stop at first illegal)
- `N`: max thinking tokens for this example
- Played move must be legal from the **original** position (ignoring the thinking block)
- Range: [0.0, 1.5]

## Forgetting Prevention

- **KL penalty** from pretrained model (standard RLVR)
- **Mixed batches**: ~30% normal play examples (no thinking block, just predict next move)

## Validation

Each generated sequence is verified with python-chess:
1. Every thinking move must be legal from the progressively updated board state
2. Played move must be legal from the original position

## Success Criteria

Phase 1 is complete when the model consistently generates:
- >90% legal moves inside thinking blocks
- >95% legal played moves
